Chapter 11: Linear Motion Formulas
• Speed (v) = Distance (d) ÷ Time (t)
• Velocity (v) = Displacement (s) ÷ Time (t)
• Acceleration (a) = (Final Velocity [v] - Initial Velocity [u]) ÷ Time [t]
